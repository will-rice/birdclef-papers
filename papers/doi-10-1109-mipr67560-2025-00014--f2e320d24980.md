## Abstract

Understanding and identifying bird species is vital for ecological research, biodiversity conservation, and environmental monitoring. However, bird identification is often difficult for non-experts, especially when relying on partial information such as bird song or species names. To address this, we propose a bird song RAG system using multimodal vector search which is a flexible retrieval system that helps users search for bird species using various types of input sound, key word, and taxonomic knowledge. In this system, Bird song audio, species names (in English and Japanese), and taxonomic data are converted into a vector in a shared multimodal space. In our experiments, taxonomic queries performed best, while bird song retrieval improved significantly through fine-tuning. From our results, careful integration of modalities is crucial, and the system provides both accessible species search and a foundation for future research in biodiversity informatics.
