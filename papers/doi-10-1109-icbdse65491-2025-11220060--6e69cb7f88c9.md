## Abstract

Bird diversity evaluation is crucial for assessing environmental impacts and habitat changes. Bird - audio based identification offers advantages like broad detection range and minimal human disturbance.ResNet18 with SE modules has achieved remarkable results in bird - audio classification tasks. Compared to image - based methods, audio - based approaches are more suitable for bird monitoring due to their wide recognition range, effectiveness in forests, and low human intrusion. Automatic acoustic identification is key for bird monitoring and protection.ResNet18 provides a solid foundation with its residual connections that alleviate gradient disappearance in deep - network training, enhancing bird - sound feature learning. The added SE module improves the model’s ability to adaptively learn feature - channel importance, emphasizing key bird - classification features and suppressing irrelevant ones, thus strengthening feature representation. The model was trained and tested on audio data from 25 bird species in the Xeno canto and Birder datasets. After careful preprocessing and feature extraction, it was fed into the Resnet18 + SE model, achieving a 95.98% test accuracy. This surpasses the performance of Vit Transformer, VGG + BiLSTM + SE, and original Resnet18 models. The Resnet18 + SE model shows great potential in bird - sound classification and can support bird monitoring and ecological research. It offers a reliable automatic identification tool for biodiversity protection and ecological assessment.
