## Abstract

Although Tamil Nadu is home to a wide variety of bird species, the absence of easily accessible tools and comprehensive data frequently makes it difficult for enthusiasts, researchers, and conservationists to identify and comprehend these species. A web program that can identify different bird species from user-uploaded images or audio recordings is being developed in order to solve this problem. Each discovered species’ physical characteristics, habitats, food, migratory patterns, and conservation statuses will all be covered in detail by the platform. The portal supports educational and conservation efforts while raising awareness of Tamil Nadu's distinctive bird species by utilizing deep learning for precise identification. AI is used here to enhance accuracy in image and audio detection, enabling efficient and reliable species recognition.
