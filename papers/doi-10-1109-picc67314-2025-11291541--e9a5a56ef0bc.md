## Abstract

Automatic classification of bird sounds is an important task in bioacoustics, supporting ecological monitoring and biodiversity conservation. This work presents a machine learning based approach for identifying bird species from their vocalizations. Various acoustic features such as Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs), spectral centroid, spectral rolloff, chroma, zero-crossing rate and energy were extracted from bird sound recordings to capture their unique audio characteristics. Dimensionality reduction techniques including Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Kernel PCA (KPCA), Sparse PCA (SPCA), and Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) were used to select the most relevant features and reduce data complexity. Several classification algorithms such as Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machines (SVM), Decision Trees, and Gaussian Process Classifiers were evaluated. Results show that high classification accuracy can be achieved with a reduced number of features, especially when LDA is used. This study emphasizes the importance of combining feature extraction and dimensionality reduction for effective and scalable bird sound classification.
