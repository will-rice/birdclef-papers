## 1 **Supplemental**

The Bioacoustic index (BI) was excluded from analysis involving added species equivalence and Figure 4 due to the non-linear correlation demonstrated by BI and the number of species present within a soundscape. Unlike the other eight indices which had a relative positive or negative linear correlation with the number of species, BI instead demonstrated a tendency to increase at intermediate numbers of species and decrease at higher and lower numbers of species (Figure A1). While it is possible to create a polynomial model using the equation BI ~ (Species)<sup>2</sup> 7 + Species, inverting the model to instead predict Species would not create a valid mathematic function.
