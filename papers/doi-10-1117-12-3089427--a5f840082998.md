## Abstract

Complex noise in urban environments severely interferes with the automatic monitoring and recognition of bird sounds, posing significant challenges to ecological conservation and biodiversity research. To address this issue, we propose a time-domain end-to-end separation model for bird sounds and urban noise, named DPECNet (Dual-Path ECA-Conformer Network), which is built on a dual-path Conformer architecture. The model incorporates an Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) mechanism into the encoder to strengthen channel-wise feature representations. In the separation module, an improved Conformer structure is employed to integrate convolutional operations with self-attention mechanisms, thereby enhancing local feature extraction and long-range temporal dependency modeling. Experiments are conducted on a constructed bird–urban noise mixture dataset, with SDRi and SI-SNRi as the primary evaluation metrics. Results demonstrate that DPECNet achieves superior denoising performance across various noise conditions, effectively recovering clean bird signals while showcasing the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed method.
