## Abstract

Bird sounds are very important to learn and research for their conservation. By listening to birds, scientists can understand how many birds in an area, and identify the types of birds in an area. This helps them to protect the birds. In this study, a computer program called BirdNET Analyzer is used. BirdNET is an intelligent (Smart) application that uses artificial intelligence (AI) to detect different sounds of birds. Instead of merely making an educated guess about what the name of the bird is, it also gives a mark to show how confident it is in its response. This enhances the right and accurate results. More information about the birds, such as where they live, whether they fly elsewhere during different parts of the year (migration), and their food, are also provided in the research. When this information is combined with sounds of birds, it may teach scientists more about the birds and learn more about each species of birds. The method is quite efficient because it is applied in order to keep track of the number of birds in different locations. It also provides early warning when the number of birds is starting to decrease and it can be a warning that the environment is in danger. Its study is also part of the United Nations goal referred to as Life on Land, which tries to protect trees, wild life and the environment. In short, this study proved that existing computer software, including BirdNET, and data about the locations, diet, and transportation of birds can help protect nature.
