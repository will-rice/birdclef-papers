## Abstract

This paper presents an Automated Avian Vocalization Classification and Geospatial Visualization System: Web-Based Based Approach to Biodiversity Monitoring to bridge the gaps of regular avian monitoring. Its objective is to design a web-based bird species identification tool from .wav audio recordings providing immediate visual and geographic reference information. The method involves creating multi-modal data sets (Xeno- Canto audio, Google Images, eBird data) transcribing audio to Mel-spectrograms, and a trained tailored ResNet-18 CNN model. The model will be incorporated into a Flask web application to show projected species pictures and interactive distribution maps. The project will be expected to provide important and readily accessible instrument for ecologists and conservationists, increasing significantly biodiversity monitoring and conservation efforts.
